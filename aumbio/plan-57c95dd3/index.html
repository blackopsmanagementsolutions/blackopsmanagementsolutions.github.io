01

Your brief

Your Marketing Pilot Starter Pack asked for a small test that can be measured: one center, one audience, one offer and a fixed advertising cap for 30 days, with every step from the first ad to a completed sale assigned, dated and measured.

This plan keeps to it:

  • AumBio owns every account, creative file, lead, customer record, domain, page and tracking pixel. We work through partner access.
  • Nothing runs without your written approval, and only the exact approved version runs.
  • The customer journey has an owner and a deadline at every step.
  • Every Monday you get a dashboard with the nine measures from your starter pack.
  • Nothing renews, expands or becomes exclusive without your written approval.

We start with people. Your starter pack names local pet owners as the working audience. In our emails of September 19 to 21 we agreed to begin with people instead. Pets can follow as a separate pilot.

The research

We read your starter pack, your National Marketing Plan and Playbook, your Franchise Disclosure Document and the 90-day marketing SOP, and we went back over our calls of September 16 and 23. We also checked your current advertising and looked at published results for campaigns like this one.

What we found

No paid ads on Sept 30Meta Ad Library, Sept 30
AumBio had no active ads on Facebook or Instagram, so the pilot gives a clean baseline for paid social.
Speed wins the leadHarvard Business Review, 2011
A study of online sales leads found that firms which followed up within an hour were nearly seven times as likely to qualify a lead as those that waited even an hour longer. The pilot sends an automated first reply within five minutes.
What leads cost elsewhereWordStream / LocaliQ, 2026
Median cost per lead on Meta: health and fitness $27.11, physicians and surgeons $32.14, beauty and personal care $50.91. A paid booking is a higher bar than the free offers behind most of these figures, so we read them as ranges, not forecasts.
Page conversionUnbounce, 2024
8.2% median conversion for wellness landing pages.
Meta's wellness rulesMeta policy
Ads may not assert or imply anything about a person's health, and Meta can restrict conversion data for wellness businesses. Your booking system therefore stays the record for bookings, sales and revenue.
TimingNovember 2026
Election Day on November 3 and the holiday season can push up the cost of reaching people, so the cost per lead may run higher than in a quieter month.

03

What we are testing

The hypothesis. A $50 introductory consultation, normally $150, shown on Facebook and Instagram to women 35 and older who live near one AumBio center, will produce completed, paid consultations at a customer acquisition cost that AumBio's package sales can recover.

The center and the audience

One center, as your starter pack sets out. We suggest Rolling Meadows, which is open six days a week and serves Arlington Heights, Palatine, Schaumburg and Hoffman Estates. Chicago on Nashville Avenue would also work. You know where the open consultation time is, so the choice is yours.

The audience is women 35 and older within that center's trade area. Targeting uses age, gender and location only. Existing clients are kept out by the offer's new-client rule, checked at booking, and your client list never leaves AumBio's systems.

The offer

A $50 introductory consultation, normally $150, for new clients at the pilot center. We recommend taking the $50 at booking, which gives people a reason to keep the appointment. From the consultation, clients move to what AumBio already sells: the package of four sessions and a follow-up, or the bundle. The offer has its own service in Mangomint, so every pilot booking and sale can be counted directly.

The messages

  1. A personalized experience, supported by technology. An hour-long first consultation built around the person in the room.
  2. Relaxation, balance and energy. The calm your site already leads with: "A Moment of Stillness in a Busy World."
  3. A simple first step. $50 for a first consultation, normally $150, booked online.

None names a condition. Each runs as both video and still ads, and the results decide which one gets the budget.

What we are building

Every step from the first ad to a completed sale, with an owner at each one.

AdBlack Ops
Four short videos cut from your existing footage, five static and carousel ads, and three fresh ads at the mid-point, all in AumBio's own ad account.
Landing pageBlack Ops
One page on an AumBio subdomain: offer terms, disclaimer, center details, a short FAQ, consent language, a callback form and a direct booking button.
Lead responseBlack Ops automates, your front desk calls
An automated text and email within five minutes, a call or text from the center within 30 minutes in opening hours, and three follow-ups over seven days.
BookingAumBio, with Black Ops
The pilot-only service in Mangomint, with confirmations, reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before, and booking status synced into GoHighLevel so follow-ups fire on real appointments.
The visitAumBio center team, with input from Liz
The consultation, the package presentation and payment, with no-shows offered a new time within 24 hours. Liz checks that the visit matches what the ads promise.
ReportingBlack Ops
Spend to lead to appointment to completed sale to revenue, every Monday, with a 30-minute call.

Behind it sit one GoHighLevel account in AumBio's name for the page, forms, texts, emails and automations; a Meta dataset with tracking that carries no health terms; scripts for phone, text and email; a 30-minute front-desk briefing; a one-page compliance card; and an ownership register handed over at the end.

07

How the pilot is judged

On completed sales and what each one cost. Leads and clicks are reported, but they are not the result. Customer acquisition cost is reported two ways: on ad spend alone, and on the full campaign cost. The Day-30 decision uses the ad-spend figure.

Scale
Acquisition cost at or under your break-even, with sales still arriving in week four. Next: a second center, then a franchisee launch kit.
Revise
Within reach of break-even, or one message clearly ahead. Next: one more month with changes.
Stop
Well above break-even. AumBio keeps every asset.

We agree the break-even with you in week one: the most AumBio will pay in ad spend to win one new client. Your own past campaign results sit beside the published figures, and the pilot is read against both.

Nothing here is a projection, forecast or guarantee of results.

Next steps

Seven decisions with you in the first week:

  1. Pilot center. We suggest Rolling Meadows or Chicago.
  2. Weekly cap. How many pilot consultations the center can take each week.
  3. Offer terms. New clients only, $50 taken at booking, what the consultation includes, whether the $50 counts toward a package, and the end date.
  4. Approver. You or a named delegate, with three business days for approvals.
  5. Call-back lead. Who at the center returns callback requests, and when.
  6. Break-even. The most AumBio will pay in ad spend to win one new client.
  7. Baseline and client profile. Results from earlier campaigns, and time with you and the center team for Liz's research.
