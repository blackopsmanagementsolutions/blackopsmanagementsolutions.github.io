About

Black Ops Management Solutions is a professional business management consulting firm focused on improving the delivery of services and enhancing the value of enterprises large and small.

Since 2009, we have earned a reputation as an established one-stop solution. We deliver innovative business strategies and client-focused solutions to independent businesses, medical clinics, private practices, and group practice models. As a hands-on advisor, we review your financial management processes, steering you toward effective business practices. We help you establish short-range and long-range financial goals, as well as strategies and plans for growth.

Our comprehensive suite of best-in-class services enables our clients to evolve their organizations, establish business processes supporting growth, improve financial performance and ultimately enhance the value of our clients’ daily operations.

The services implemented through the Black Ops platform, including expertise resident within our firm and through established relationships with our service partners, include:

Private Practice

We use our extensive industry expertise and hands-on approach to help Private Practitioner achieve their business goals. We provide our clients with a comprehensive understanding of practice performance and identify opportunities for improvement. Working directly with dentists, we help set provider goals, streamline collections, and create tailored KPIs to drive practice success.

Cosmetic Medicine

Our team provides keen financial insights to private practices, MSOs, medical spas, and plastic surgeons. We help our clients understand their practice health through the financial performance of each provider, service, and product. We also work with clients to implement business strategies, such as adding providers, expanding services, or remaining compliant with an MSO model.

Small Businesses

We offer financial services to a full spectrum of small businesses, including law firms, veterinary offices, marketing agencies, chiropractors, construction companies, and more. Our team works closely with yours to provide strategic guidance and actionable financial insights to improve daily operations.

Lynn’s professional experience also includes formal leadership training incorporating contemporary management approaches, which has given him a keen understanding of the motivations, concerns, and aspirations of owners, managers, professionals, and staff throughout medical organizations.
