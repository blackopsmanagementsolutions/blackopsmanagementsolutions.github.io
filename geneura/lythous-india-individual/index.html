02 / Where you are
Where you are

Recognise yourself in any of these?

  • “I don’t think anything is wrong.”
  • “Something is wrong. I can’t name it.”
  • “I know what I need. But I can’t get to it.”
  • “I’m just curious.”

One of them, or all four. Either way, you can come in. Plenty of people walk in only to find out what a Lythous node is, or to ask about becoming an Ambassador. You do not need a reason that sounds serious enough.

03 / You are welcome here
You are welcome here

You do not need a problem to have a possibility.

You may not know what you need. You may not see what is holding you back. You may not yet know what is possible.

That is where the light begins. Nobody asks you what is wrong with you. We start with who you are, what matters to you, and what you would like to change.

We do not organise care around what we offer. We organise what we offer around you.

We begin by asking:

  • Who are you?
  • What matters to you?
  • What is possible for you?
  • Who do you want to become?
04 / Unsure
If you are hesitating

Are you hesitating? Unsure? Not sure what to say? Afraid of being judged?

No problem at all. This is how most people begin with a Lythous node.

You do not have to prepare anything, or work out what to call it first. You can say “I do not really know why I am here” and that is a fine place to start. You can bring a friend. You can say nothing at all for a while.

  • No preparation
  • No appointment
  • No paperwork
  • No waiting list

What you share is yours

You decide what you tell us, and how much. You can ask at any point what has been written down and who can see it. Nothing about what happens next is decided without you.

You can stop at any point, and come back later.

Nothing happens to you that you did not agree to.

Ways in

An Ambassador

An Ambassador is a trained person whose job is to notice. On campus, in a cafeteria, in a bookshop, at the door of a Lythous node.

What they do

  • They notice the person who is always on their own, or who stopped turning up
  • They say hello, and ask what would help today
  • They walk in with you rather than pointing at a door
  • They stay with you until your journey is complete

What they do not do

An Ambassador does not diagnose you and does not treat you. They connect you to the right people and stay alongside while you get there.

Ways in

A Listener

A Listener is the first human you reach. Not a chatbot, not a menu, not a form. A person.

Why it matters

At three in the morning, somebody who is frightened should reach a human being. That is the whole point of it.

Call, text or message Healthcare SMC India on +91 96346 76724. If you are in immediate danger, call 112, or Tele-MANAS on 14416.

Ways in

Walk into a room

A Lythous node is a room on campus with people in it. You can walk in without an appointment and without telling anyone why.

What is inside

  • A reception, with an Ambassador who says hello
  • A safe room, to get away from the noise, or to make a private call
  • A reflection room, quiet and low lit, with nothing expected of you
  • A clinical room, for when a doctor is the right person
  • A common area, for whatever that campus needs it to be

Every node is fitted to the place it is in, so no two are the same.

Ways in

Online

Walking into a room is a lot to ask of some people on some days. You do not have to.

Call, text or message and you reach the same people. Where a campus has a virtual Lythous node, you can start there and come in later, or never come in at all.

Whatever your comfort is, that is the right way in.

Ways in

Someone you know

You do not have to be the one who makes contact. A friend, a classmate, a teacher, a warden, a family member, anybody in your community can start it with you or for you.

Faculty and Ambassadors work together, so a teacher who is worried about somebody has a real person to take that to.

A written protocol covers anyone assessed as being at risk. It names who is contacted, in what order, and how quickly.
