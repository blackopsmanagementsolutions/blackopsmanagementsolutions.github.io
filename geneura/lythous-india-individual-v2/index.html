What happens next

After you say hello.

  1. 01
    Recognition

    You reach out, or an Ambassador notices and invites you. Either way, it starts with a person, not a form.

  2. 02
    Engagement

    You talk, on your terms. In the room, or by phone, text or WhatsApp.

  3. 03
    Navigation

    Together you work out what would actually help, and who already near you can give it.

  4. 04
    Escalation

    If you are ever at risk, a written plan says who acts, in what order, and how fast. You are told what it is.

  5. 05
    Follow-through

    Your Ambassador goes with you until you have arrived, and somebody checks back afterwards.

FAQs

Common questions you may have.

What if I do not know what to say?

You do not have to know. “I do not really know why I am here” is a fine place to start, and you can say nothing at all for a while.

Will I be judged?

No. Nobody asks you what is wrong with you.

What if it is not serious enough?

There is no bar to clear. Curiosity is enough.

How long does this take?

Come when it suits you. Stay as long as you like.

Is there a form to fill in?

No. It is a conversation, over however long it takes.

Will it be written down?

You decide what you tell us. You can ask at any point what has been written down, and who can see it.

Can I bring someone, or stop?

Bring anyone you like. Stop whenever you want, and come back later.

An Ambassador

An Ambassador is a trained person whose job is to notice. On campus, in a cafeteria, in a bookshop, at the door of a Lythous™ node.

What they do

  • They notice the person who is always on their own, or who stopped turning up
  • They say hello, and ask what would help today
  • They walk in with you rather than pointing at a door
  • They stay with you until your journey is complete

What they do not do

An Ambassador does not diagnose you and does not treat you. They connect you to the right people and stay alongside while you get there.

A Listener

A Listener is the first human you reach. A person.

Why it matters

At three in the morning, somebody who is frightened should reach a human being. That is the whole point of it.

Walk into a room

A Lythous™ node is a room on campus with people in it. You can walk in without an appointment and without telling anyone why.

What is inside

  • A reception, with an Ambassador who says hello
  • A safe room, to get away from the noise, or to make a private call
  • A reflection room, quiet and low lit, with nothing expected of you
  • A clinical room, for when a doctor is the right person
  • A common area, for whatever that campus needs it to be

Every node is fitted to the place it is in, so no two are the same.
