I Need Help

Individual

Purpose: Written for a frightened nineteen-year-old, in a completely different register. Short sentences, no institutional language, and nothing that reads like a form.

1Talk to someone now

Text, WhatsApp, call, walk in. Above everything else on the page.

2What happens when you reach out

See → Connect → Engage → Support → Belong, in student words.

3You don’t have to know what’s wrong

Removes the entry barrier that stops most people.

4What this is and isn’t

“Nobody here will diagnose you or treat you. We listen, and we help you get to the right person.”

5What we write down

The most important trust line on the site for a student.

6Where to find us

The space, the hours and a photograph, so that arriving is not a leap.

7For families

How to help someone you’re worried about.

8If it’s urgent

Crisis numbers, prominent.
